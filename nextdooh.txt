Platform Capabilities
Built for operators
who move fast.
01
Device Fleet Management
Register and operate unlimited Android displays from a single dashboard. Portrait, landscape, 4K — any orientation, any resolution, any location.

02
Rich Media Library
Upload images and videos. Build playlists that auto-sync the moment content changes. Zero manual intervention required on the device side.

03
Multi-Zone Layouts
Divide any screen into vertical zones. Run a live exchange-rate ticker beside a product video wall — simultaneously, in perfect sync.

04
Instant Content Push
Hit save. Content propagates to every paired display in under two seconds over WebSocket. No polling. No delays. Real broadcast speed.

05
Web App Integration
Embed any URL into a zone. Live dashboards, restaurant menus, weather widgets, news tickers — if it runs in a browser, it runs on your screen.

06
Remote Device Control
Reboot devices, schedule power on/off windows, and push firmware updates — all without physical access. Full kiosk-mode lockdown supported.

07
Real-Time Sync Protocol
WebSocket-powered two-way communication keeps every device in perfect sync with the server. Reconnects automatically after any network interruption.

08
Zero-Trust Security
JWT device tokens, encrypted local storage, HTTPS-only API, per-account content isolation. Built for operators who cannot afford a breach.

How It Works
Live in minutes.
Not days.
01
Upload Media
Drag and drop images or videos into your media library. Organise, reorder, and set per-item display durations in seconds.

02
Design a Layout
Use the zone editor to split your screen. Assign media playlists, web apps, or live tickers to each zone independently.

03
Pair Your Screen
Install the NextDOOH app on any Android TV or display. Scan the QR code once — the device pairs and starts playing instantly.

04
Push & Monitor
Update content from anywhere in the world. Watch devices come online, push new layouts, and reboot remotely if needed.
